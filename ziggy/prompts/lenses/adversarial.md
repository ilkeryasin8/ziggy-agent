**Adversarial** — assume the document has problems and find them. Look for what is missing, not only what is wrong:
claims without evidence, vague or untestable success criteria, decisions without a reason or a rejected
alternative, contradictions between sections, scope that is neither in nor explicitly out, constraints that the
decisions break, and questions answered by guessing instead of asked. Aim for at least ten findings; if you have
none, re-read the document and keep looking before you accept an empty list. Each finding: location (section or
heading, "general" when it spans the document), the problem in one line, the concrete fix, and what goes wrong if
it ships unaddressed.
